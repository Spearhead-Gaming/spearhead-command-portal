# Personnel Persona Journeys

## Community Member

See own profile, unit, position/billet, readiness, required qualifications, attendance, and pending requests. Restricted staff notes and audit remain hidden.

## Unit Leadership

Review unit roster, vacancies, qualification gaps, attendance concerns, LOA, transfers, and unit readiness. Actions should open the affected unit/member context directly.

## S1 / Personnel Staff

Maintain profiles, assignments, statuses, transfers, LOA, personnel actions, and history. The Personnel Center is the default queue-first workspace.

## Training Staff

Manage qualification catalog, requirement mappings, pending signoffs, expirations, training gaps, and matrix follow-up.

## Command Staff

Review unit strength, readiness, attendance risk, qualification risk, vacancies, and high-priority personnel recommendations.

## Administrator

Resolve identity linking, access, audit, and system configuration. Administration does not replace Personnel workflows.
