# Glossary

## Spearhead Command Portal
The custom management platform for Spearhead Gaming.

## Discord First
A design approach where Discord remains the primary user interaction layer.

## Source of Truth
The authoritative place where official data is stored.

## ORBAT
Order of Battle. A structured representation of unit organization.

## CONOP
Concept of Operations. A mission planning document.

## AAR
After Action Review. A post-operation review.

## S1
Personnel staff section.

## S2
Intelligence staff section.

## S3
Operations staff section.

## S4
Logistics staff section.

## RASP
Ranger Assessment and Selection Program, used for 75th Ranger Regiment selection workflow.

## Qualification Matrix
A grid showing which members hold which qualifications.
