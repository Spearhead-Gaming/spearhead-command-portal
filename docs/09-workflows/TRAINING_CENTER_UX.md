# Training Center UX

Training Center should prioritize active work:

- Upcoming training.
- Pending signoffs.
- Members needing training.
- Expiring qualifications.
- Overdue requirements.
- Unit training gaps.
- Instructor workload.

Historical training events, instructor history, and analytics stay secondary.
# Discord Scheduled Events

Training events may create Discord Scheduled Event plans for the Primary Community Guild or owning Unit guild when policy allows. Discord interest does not award qualifications and does not finalize attendance.
