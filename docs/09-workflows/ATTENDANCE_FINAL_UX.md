# Attendance Final UX

Attendance is unit-scoped and date-range explicit.

Default view: selected unit, selected date range, attendance summary, members below policy, missing records, recent events, excused absences, LOA exclusions, and trend.

Full event-by-event history, correction audit, policy configuration, and community aggregate are secondary.
# Discord Event Interest Boundary

Discord Scheduled Event interest may appear as a staff review signal, but it must not finalize attendance. Portal RSVP, confirmed participation, voice suggestions, and finalized attendance are separate states.
