# Case Management Workflow

## Purpose

This workflow defines how staff create, assign, review, decide, close, reopen, and archive cases.

## Flow

1. Staff creates a case or an incident intake creates one.
2. Case receives a unique case number.
3. Staff assigns the case to a user, permission queue, or unit-scoped staff.
4. Staff adds notes, participants, and evidence as needed.
5. Staff moves the case through valid transitions.
6. Staff records a decision or resolution.
7. Case is resolved, dismissed, appealed, closed, or archived.

## Timeline

The case timeline records important events:

- case created
- assignment changed
- status changed
- note added
- evidence added
- decision recorded
- warning issued
- moderation action requested or completed
- appeal submitted
- case closed or reopened

## Safety

Do not hard-delete case history. Confidentiality must be enforced before data reaches UI components.
