# Moderation Workflow

## Purpose

Moderation is one part of community management. Moderation actions should be requested, recorded, and audited through the portal.

## Flow

1. Staff opens or creates a moderation case.
2. Staff records evidence and reason.
3. Staff records a decision or requests a moderation action.
4. Supported Discord actions execute through the Discord moderation provider.
5. Unsupported actions remain recorded as provider-pending.
6. Result is added to the case timeline and audit log.
7. Communication uses the Unified Communication Pipeline where notice is required.

## Supported Execution

Kick is supported when Discord bot credentials and permissions are configured.

Ban, timeout, remove timeout, and unban are recorded as provider-pending until their providers are implemented.
