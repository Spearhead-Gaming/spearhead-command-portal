# Qualification Requirements

## Purpose

Qualification requirements define unit- and position-scoped readiness expectations.

## Requirement Sources

- Assigned unit
- Assigned position or billet
- Explicit member requirement, future placeholder

## Requirement States

- Completed
- Due Soon
- Overdue
- Expiring
- Exempt
- Not Applicable

## Rules

- Do not duplicate requirement calculations in UI components.
- Do not hardcode unit-specific due windows in the UI.
- Show missing requirements on member profiles, unit readiness, and qualification matrix views.
