# Personnel Handoffs

Personnel workflows cross personas. The portal should make the next owner clear without exposing restricted notes.

## Canonical Handoffs

1. Member submits LOA, transfer, or profile correction.
2. Unit leadership reviews operational impact.
3. S1 performs administrative review and applies assignment/status/profile changes.
4. Training staff resolves qualification/signoff readiness gaps.
5. Command reviews high-impact readiness and vacancy issues.
6. Administrators resolve identity/access issues.

My Work and Personnel Center should point directly to the responsible queue or affected record.
