# Personnel & Readiness Center Community Workflow

## Community Intent

The Personnel & Readiness Center gives members, unit leadership, S1, training staff, and command one shared place to answer:

- Who belongs to each unit?
- What status is each member in?
- What qualifications or attendance concerns need attention?
- Which transfers, LOA requests, or personnel actions need review?

## Spearhead Assumptions

- Discord display name is the primary visible name.
- Rank is optional and not a primary community structure.
- Unit, billet, status, qualifications, attendance, and readiness carry the operational meaning.
- Discord supports identity and notifications; the portal remains authoritative.

## Workflow Surfaces

- `/personnel` for overview and queues.
- `/personnel/members` for member search and inspection.
- `/personnel/roster` for assignment actions.
- `/units` for unit dashboards.
- `/training/qualification-matrix` for qualification gap review.

## Review Workflows

Transfer and LOA requests should create review records and related case-engine context when a staff decision is required. Assignment changes happen only after approval.
