# Discord Gateway Voice Awareness

Voice awareness is informational only.

## Allowed

- Observe voice joins, moves, and leaves.
- Store non-authoritative presence sessions.
- Surface staff awareness about current Discord voice activity.

## Not Allowed

- Do not infer attendance.
- Do not record audio.
- Do not create disciplinary or readiness decisions from voice presence alone.
- Do not expose private member activity beyond staff-authorized diagnostics.

## Required Intent

`GuildVoiceStates`
