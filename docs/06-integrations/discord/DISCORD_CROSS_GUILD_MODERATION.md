# Discord Cross-Guild Moderation

Spearhead may manage one primary community guild and multiple unit guilds.

## Principle

Cross-guild moderation is policy-driven and never automatic by assumption.

## Policy Modes

Guild policy can be used to represent:

- view-only history
- staff notification
- mirror warning recommendation
- mirror timeout recommendation
- mirror kick recommendation
- mirror ban recommendation
- manual review

The Portal may recommend cross-guild action, but staff must explicitly approve and execute enforcement unless future policy code safely enables another mode.

## Source Of Truth

The Portal case is the shared history. Discord guilds remain enforcement targets only.
