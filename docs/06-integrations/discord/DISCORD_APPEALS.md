# Discord Moderation Appeals

Appeals remain attached to the original `CommunityCase`.

## Lifecycle

- Submitted
- Under Review
- Additional Information
- Accepted
- Denied
- Closed

## Rules

- Appeal review requires `discord.moderation.appeals` or the broader case permissions used by the Community Management module.
- Appeal decisions should update the case timeline.
- Accepted appeals may lead to a reversal action such as unban or timeout removal, but reversal must still use the case-backed moderation service.
